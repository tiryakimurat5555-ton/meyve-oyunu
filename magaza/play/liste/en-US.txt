Prism Orchard
Match glossy crystal fruits in a cheerful garden. Hundreds of levels!
Welcome to Prism Orchard, a bright and cheerful match-3 puzzle game full of smiling, glossy fruits!

Swap neighboring fruits to line up three or more of the same kind. Strawberries, lemons, grapes, oranges, apples and plums pop with sparkling crystal sounds.

SPECIAL FRUITS
• Match 4 to make a striped fruit that clears a whole row or column.
• Match in an L or T shape to make a bomb.
• Match 5 in a row to make a rainbow fruit that clears every fruit of one color.
• Combine special fruits for huge garden blasts!

A NEW SURPRISE IN EVERY GARDEN
Hundreds of levels across many themed gardens: frozen fruits, crates, snow, seeds to bring down, vines that grow, barrels, fog, flowers to pollinate, ticking clocks, windy conveyor rows, chicks to rescue, balloons, honey, frogs, lanterns, space gardens with changing gravity and much more. Every garden brings a brand new mechanic.

HELPERS
Use the hammer, shuffle and extra moves boosters when you get stuck, and collect a daily reward basket every day.

FOR EVERYONE
• No lives and no waiting: play as much as you like.
• Relaxed difficulty that grows slowly.
• Fully playable with VoiceOver and TalkBack screen readers: every fruit and goal is described.

Start your journey through the orchard today!
